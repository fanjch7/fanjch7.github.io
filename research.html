Research

Publications

  1. Information Dissemination and the Monetary Policy Uncertainty Premium: Evidence from China

    Jianhao Lin, Jiacheng Fan, Yifan Zhang. Journal of Banking & Finance, 2025, 171, 107371. [Paper]

    This paper proposes a novel monetary policy uncertainty measure aggregating information from policy communication, policy operation and news coverage. Evidence from the Chinese equity market indicates that stocks providing a hedge against this uncertainty earn lower returns, consistent with a negative uncertainty risk premium.

  2. Real-time Macroeconomic Projection Using Narrative Central Bank Communication

    Jianhao Lin, Jiacheng Fan, Yifan Zhang, Liangyuan Chen. Journal of Applied Econometrics, 2023, 38(2), 202–221. [Paper] [Replication code]

    This paper incorporates predictive signals extracted from central bank communications into mixed-frequency macroeconomic forecasting models. These text-based signals improve forecast accuracy in real-time, out-of-sample evaluations.

Working Papers

  1. Designing Leadership under Social Conformity

    Jiacheng Fan. 2026. [Paper]

    This paper studies how to select leaders and design hierarchies in a network game with social conformity. Optimal groups favor locally productive players under weak conformity but collapse to a single, most productive leader under strong conformity; finer sequencing need not raise aggregate effort.

  2. Information Acquisition and the Value of Public Information in Network Games

    Jiacheng Fan. 2026 · Under review at Journal of Economic Theory. [Paper] [Presentation]

    This paper models costly private information acquisition in a network beauty contest. More precise public information crowds out the acquisition of private information most sharply among central agents, reducing the private and social value of public information relative to exogenous private information.

  3. Differentiated Policy Communication with Heterogenous Agents

    Liangyuan Chen, Nanan Chen, Jiacheng Fan, Jianhao Lin. 2026. [Paper]

    This paper compares common and differentiated signals for experts and the public. Precise expert signals paired with simpler public messages improve welfare by preserving expert accuracy while reducing interpretation costs.

Work in Progress

  1. When Less is More: Optimal Targeting of Public Communication in Networks

    Jiacheng Fan, Xuesong Huang, Xueheng Li, Jianhao Lin. 2026. [Presentation]

    This paper studies whom a planner should inform in a network. Broadcasting is often optimal, but targeting can improve welfare when central and influential recipients amplify noisy public signals.

Research in Chinese Journals

Publications

  1. Narrative Asset Pricing with Large Language Models

    Jiacheng Fan, Jianhao Lin, Zongyu Li. China Economic Quarterly, 2026, No. 3. [Paper] [Appendix]

    This paper uses BERT to extract news narratives and construct narrative pricing factors. These factors explain asset pricing anomalies better than traditional stock-characteristic factors and deliver stronger out-of-sample investment performance.

  2. Text Analysis and Trading Strategies with Multi-Source Financial Large Language Models

    Jiacheng Fan, Junfan Wu, Jianhao Lin. China Journal of Econometrics, 2026, No. 2. [Paper]

    This paper combines news, stock prices, and macroeconomic information into trading signals generated by large language models. High-buy-score portfolio strategies perform strongly, and fine-tuning on brokerage research reports further improves returns.

  3. Information Frictions, Public Data and Firms' Innovation Boundaries

    Jiasong Xie, Jiacheng Fan, Jianhao Lin. Economic Research Journal, 2025, No. 11. [Paper]

    This paper develops an innovation choice model with incomplete information and tests its predictions using the rollout of public data platforms. Public data promotes breakthrough innovation by improving information provision and reducing coordination costs.

  4. Words and Deeds of Central Bank, Monetary Policy Uncertainty and Asset Pricing

    Yifan Zhang, Jianhao Lin, Guangzhong Li, Jiacheng Fan. Journal of Management Sciences in China, 2025, Accepted.

    This paper measures monetary policy uncertainty from gaps between central bank communication and policy actions. These gaps widen during exits from monetary easing and carry a significantly negative risk premium in the cross-section of stock returns.

  5. Measuring Innovation Activities in China's Listed Companies: A Text-Based Approach

    Jiasong Xie, Jiacheng Fan, Jianhao Lin. Statistical Research, 2025, No. 2. [Paper]

    This paper constructs text-based corporate innovation measures from annual reports, covering firms without patents or R&D data and capturing information missed by traditional innovation measures.

  6. Central Bank Communication and Policy Interest Rate Transmission: A Study Based on Open Market Operation

    Yifan Zhang, Jiacheng Fan, Jianhao Lin, Hongxiang Lin. Statistical Research, 2024, No. 12. [Paper]

    This paper examines the communication effects of open market operation announcements. Additional information in these announcements strengthens policy rate transmission and reduces banks' disagreement about short-term liquidity.

  7. News Data and Real-Time Consumption Growth Projections: A View from Narrative Economics

    Yifan Zhang, Jianhao Lin, Jiacheng Fan. Journal of Financial Research, 2023, No. 5. [Paper]

    This paper constructs a news-based consumption sentiment index and uses it for real-time consumption growth forecasting.

Working Papers

  1. From Spillovers to Breakthroughs: Digital Knowledge Flows and Firms’ Innovation Boundaries

    Jiasong Xie, Jiacheng Fan. 2026 · The Journal of World Economy, Accepted. [Paper]

    This paper combines an innovation choice model with patent citations and text data to study digital knowledge flows. Digital knowledge improves information precision and lowers the cost of applying knowledge, strengthening absorptive capacity and helping firms move beyond existing technological paths.
